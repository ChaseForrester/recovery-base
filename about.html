Hi, we're Julie and Nathan

Healthy doesn’t have to be hard.

We’re the founders of Recovery Base, a space built from our own journey with health, training, and recovery.

Born and raised in the Illawarra, we wanted to create something that felt local, real, and inclusive — a calm sanctuary where every body can feel supported to restore, recharge, and reconnect.

Contact

Our story

Our health and wellness journey began at home.

Like many families, life for us has always been full — juggling work, kids, and finding time to move, rest, and look after ourselves. Over the years, we realised something was missing: a space that made recovery easy, accessible, and part of everyday life.

For Julie, health and wellbeing have always been a passion. From the gym to self-care, she’s seen how simple recovery practices can completely change how we feel in our bodies and minds.

For Nathan, recovery is deeply personal. After years of back issues, surgeries, and chronic pain, he saw first-hand how hard it can be to find the right support. That experience planted the seed for Recovery Base — a place where recovery isn’t just for athletes, but for anyone who wants to live with less pain and more energy.

What we’re building is more than a business. It’s a community space where people come together to slow down, feel better, and take care of themselves in ways that fit everyday life.

How it works at Recovery Base

1

Choose how you’d like to recover

Prefer a little solitude? Go private. Feeling social? Try the communal space. Saunas, ice baths and floats, in your own rhythm.

2

Step inside and slow down

Take your shoes and socks off. From the moment you walk in, the pace shifts. The air feels calmer, and the space feels grounded.

3

Settle in and breathe

We’ll guide you through what to expect. There’s no rush and no rules. This is your time — silence, stillness, or connection.

4

Leave feeling lighter

You’ll walk out refreshed, grounded, and a little more connected to yourself. You can come back to this feeling anytime.
