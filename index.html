FAQs

Got questions? You’re not alone.

Here are the ones we hear most. Still wondering? Send a note or call us.

How do I know if Recovery Base is for me?

If you're feeling tired, stressed, sore, or just a little disconnected from your body, Recovery Base is for you. You don’t need to be an athlete or have experience with recovery tools. Whether you’re training hard, navigating everyday stress, or looking for space to reset, you’ll be supported here.

What’s the difference between communal and private sessions?

Communal sessions are shared spaces — ice baths and saunas — where several people recover at the same time. Private sessions give you the room to yourself, ideal if you’re after more quiet, privacy, or focus.

I’ve never done an ice bath or float tank before. Will someone help me?

Yes. You’ll never be left guessing. The team walks you through each modality so you feel safe, supported, and in control the whole time.

Can I just come in once to try it out?

Yes. The first communal contrast therapy session is free. After that you can start with a casual pass, with no commitment. Many people begin this way before moving into a membership.

What’s included in a membership?

Memberships give you access at a weekly rate, with flexible options based on how often you want to come in. Choose communal or private sessions, and adjust your plan anytime.

Do I need to bring anything?

Private rooms include towels and everything you need. Bring yourself, a water bottle, and wear something comfortable. For communal sessions, please bring your own towel. Avoid caffeine beforehand, and don’t come in after drinking alcohol.
