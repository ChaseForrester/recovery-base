Your complete guide to recovery: the tools that help every body

At Recovery Base Shellharbour, recovery isn’t one-size-fits-all. It’s a combination of rest and practical tools designed to help every body feel and function well.

Whether you’re an athlete chasing performance, managing stress, or simply looking to move better and live well, the space is built to support you physically and mentally.

Sauna therapy

The warmth of a sauna does more than relax muscles. It promotes circulation, reduces stress, and helps your body and mind unwind. We offer both traditional and infrared saunas, so you can choose deep dry heat or a gentler infrared warmth. Regular sauna use is one of the simplest recovery tools we have.

Read the complete guide to sauna recovery

Ice baths

Cold water therapy helps your body adapt to stress, reduce inflammation, and accelerate healing. Communal and private ice baths make cold immersion accessible. Paired with heat — contrast therapy — the benefits multiply.

Read the complete guide to ice bath recovery

Compression therapy

The compression lounge uses boots that apply rhythmic pressure to your legs, stimulating blood flow, reducing swelling, and supporting your lymphatic system. It’s useful after training and after long hours on your feet.

Read the complete guide to compression therapy

Float therapy

Float therapy is a quiet, weightless hour. Warm water and Epsom salts create effortless buoyancy, deep relaxation, and a calmer nervous system. People use it for sleep, anxiety, and a pause from everyday noise.

Read the complete guide to float therapy

How it comes together

Heat and cold work together to boost circulation and build resilience. Compression supports the hours between sessions. Float therapy calms the mind. You don’t need all of them at once. Start with the one that feels approachable, and build a rhythm from there.
